About My Overtime

My Overtime is a simple and easy-to-use personal overtime calculator made especially for garment worker brothers and sisters.

Keeping track of how many hours you worked overtime and how much money you should receive can sometimes be difficult. My Overtime helps you keep your own overtime records in a simple, organized, and convenient way.

Your Privacy Matters
My Overtime does not require unnecessary access to your phone. The App does not need your photos, contacts, or other personal phone data for its purpose.

The information you enter is used for maintaining and calculating your own overtime records.

Made for Garment Workers

The main purpose of My Overtime is to help garment workers easily save and manage their own overtime records and keep track of their hard-earned overtime payments.

We hope My Overtime will make your daily overtime calculation easier and more convenient. My Overtime — Your Work, Your Records, Your Control.
